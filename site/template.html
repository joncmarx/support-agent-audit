    Limits

    • An AI model does the grading. It grades against the accepted answer, and its reasoning for every reply is shown above so you can check it.
    • The "needs a human" labels are also set by an AI model, before any bot ran, using a written rule. They decide only whether a handoff counts as correct or unnecessary.
    • Each version ran once on each question. Small differences, such as 1 versus 4 wrong answers, are within run-to-run noise. The large ones are not.
    • Forum questions are harder than a typical support queue: the easy ones get answered by the docs and never reach the forum.
